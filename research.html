Research Data

My project was a meta-analysis of four peer-reviewed studies, each coded for four themes: AI’s technological advancement, its limits in predictive power, ethical/social implications, and the lack of comprehensive datasets plus the black-box problem. The studies, coding framework, and supporting figures are presented within my final paper.
